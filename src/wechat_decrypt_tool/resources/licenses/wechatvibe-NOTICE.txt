Changes: Python adaptation, existing application's six-axis field names,
Chinese broad-label presentation, explicit 50% message-label display threshold,
per-message source preservation, target-scoped statistical summaries with
unknown MBTI axes, no fabricated empty-input score or model-failure fallback.
It is not a port of WechatVibe's complete fine-grained label catalogue.

ai/insight_local_fine.py additionally adapts the generic-v9 intent catalogue,
candidate retrieval expressions and selection flow from:
https://github.com/tswawa/WechatVibe/blob/78446c00dc0d0e014a5a51f86ab000d5848051fe/electron/laya/general-intent.ts
and the emotion/intent decision separation from local-message-insights.ts and
laya/options.ts at the same revision. Original source uses Apache-2.0.

Changes: Python implementation, display names limited to four characters,
additional emotion and intent candidates, model-only label selection, strict
probability validation, complete context and explicit weak-portrait prior,
source-bound labels and versioned raw question/answer records. Candidate cues
retrieve choices only; they never determine displayed labels or probabilities.
This module does not copy the separate 547-intent hierarchical catalogue.
